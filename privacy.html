Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Lumara ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Lumara app.

Information you provide

Account: when you sign up, we store your email address and authentication details through our authentication provider (Supabase).

Profile and onboarding: preferences you enter, such as goals and routine details, used to personalize the app.

Treatment data: the treatments, providers, dates, notes, and spending you record.

Photos: images you add to document your journey. Photos are associated with your account so the app can show them back to you.

Information collected automatically

Subscription status: when you buy or restore Lumara Elite, our payments provider (RevenueCat) and Apple process the purchase and report entitlement status. We receive whether your subscription is active, not your payment card details.

Basic app diagnostics needed to keep the service working. We do not sell your personal information.

How we use your information

To provide the app: store and display your treatments, providers, photos, and insights.

To manage your subscription and unlock premium features.

To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

How your information is stored and shared

Your data is stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) on your behalf. We share information only with providers that operate the service — Supabase (authentication and database), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Apple (payments) — and only as needed for those functions.

We do not share your treatment data or photos with advertisers.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your associated data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep records to meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You can access and edit your information in the app. You can request a copy of your data or its deletion by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support@lumaraelite.com. We will confirm and process the request.

Children

Lumara is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: support@lumaraelite.com.
