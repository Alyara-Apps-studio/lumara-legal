Managing your subscription

Lumara Elite is billed through your Apple Account. To view, change, or cancel your subscription, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also open it directly from the Lumara app: Profile → Subscription → Manage Subscription.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstalled Lumara or switched devices, open the paywall in the app and tap Restore Purchases to bring back your Lumara Elite access.

Deleting your account or data

To delete your account and its associated data, email support@lumaraelite.com from the address on your account. We will confirm and process the request.

Reporting a problem

Found a bug, or something not working as expected? Email support@lumaraelite.com with a description of what happened and, if you can, a screenshot.
